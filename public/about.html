About Holly Lineback

Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor

Background

Holly Lineback, CMHC
Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor, State of Utah

Education: Masters Degree from the University of Utah, Educational Psychology & Mental Health

Email: dhlineback@gmail.com / Phone: 801-259-7311 (text, voice msg)

Experience: I opened my private practice Feb. 1, 2011. Before that I worked at schools and community agencies for 15 years.

Current areas of emphasis: All types of anxiety, worry, depression, and stressful life situations and/or transitions.

Concerns I Address

  • Anxiety: worry, social, panic, etc.
  • Sadness and Depression
  • Grief and life losses
  • Stressful life situations
  • Relationship problems; friends, family, etc. (individually, I don't see couples or families.)
  • Life challenges, such as aging, career, grief, finances, health, and relationships, etc.
  • Self and Identity issues: questioning & exploring one's identity, values, spirituality, meaning, joy and fulfillment in life.

Therapy Approaches

I collaborate with the individual to meet their needs. Coming from a foundation that includes: person-centered, attachment, relational, and psycho-educational perspectives.

  • ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy)
  • CBT (Cognitive-Behavioral therapy)
  • MCBT (Mindfulness Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT)
  • SFBT (Solution focused brief therapy)
