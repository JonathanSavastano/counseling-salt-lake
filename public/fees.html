Counseling Fees & Insurance

Payment options for therapy in Salt Lake City, Utah

Payment Options

Self-pay

You pay the fee at the time of service, and there is no insurance, or other 3rd party billing required of me.

A self-pay fee is $95.

Sliding scale based on need, resources, room in my schedule, and other factors.

Insurance

If you are insured, your out-of-pocket costs (co-pay, deductible, etc.) are determined by your plan. Insurance companies often have several plan choices that individuals and companies can choose from, with varying costs and benefits. Insurance is confusing, but I can help you figure it out.

Medicare

I am on Medicare and many of the Supplemental (medigap) and Advantage plans, though not all. (I enjoy working with older adults.)

HSA Accounts

These are becoming more common, especially when combined with high-deductible medical plans. The employer and the employee pay into them, and you can use them to pay your out-of-pocket costs (co-payment or deductible). They cover any medical costs, including mental health, and often other related medical costs as well.

Insurance / Managed Care

The insurance and the health care system is complicated and confusing! I can help with that if you provide me with your insurance information and date of birth.
