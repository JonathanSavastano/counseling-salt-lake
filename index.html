Experienced, accessible, in-person connection for adults dealing with stress, anxiety and life issues.

There are times in everyone's life when problems can become overwhelming and interfere with our ability to function well and feel good. It can affect our work, relationships, and most importantly---our own joy and peace of mind.

If you are stressed, worried, anxious, etc., it can help to consult with an experienced, approachable, warm and practical therapist. I want you to feel heard, understood and validated. I do my best to create a safe and comfortable atmosphere. Together we can look at sensitive issues in a non-judgmental way. I can offer perspective, challenge, insight, support, hope, and an impartial viewpoint in a confidential setting.

I help adults dealing with:

  • Anxiety, worry, emotional overwhelm
  • Irritability and emotional reactivity
  • Sadness, grief, depression
  • Stressful life circumstances
  • Relationship difficulties and unhealthy patterns
  • Life transitions, from the early 20's and midlife and older adults adjusting to being retired

Counseling is not always quick or easy, but for most people it can provide relief and lead to positive change. No one is without challenges at some point in their life. Get some support, gain perspective on your situation, and start feeling better.

I mostly work in person and think it's more effective, but online can be used as a backup in certain situations.
